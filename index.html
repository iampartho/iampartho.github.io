Partho Ghosh

Medical computer vision for the lungs: making AI work where labeled data is scarce.

I’m a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Iowa, advised by Dr. Sarah E. Gerard in the Pulmonary Imaging & Computer Vision (PICV) Lab. I build deep-learning tools for pulmonary analysis, and I’m especially interested in developing models that can generalize across modalities, achieving robust performance in data scarce modalities by transferring what models learn from data-rich modalities.

Outside the lab you’ll find me travelling, hiking, playing sports, or switching off with music.
